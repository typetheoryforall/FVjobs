Formal verification hiring, week by week

How many roles the board tracks, and how many of them employers posted each week. The numbers come from the board's own history, updated with every search run; the last update was on {{updated}}.

The board over time

Open roles on the board on each day its data changed.

Early jumps are the board catching up: each deep search found roles that had been open for months. Growth from week to week is a better sign of the market once the searches start finding mostly new postings.

New postings per week

The tracked roles counted by the week their employer posted them, over the last {{weeks}} weeks, by kind of verification.

    {{legend}}

{{in_window}} roles fall in these weeks; {{earlier}} more were posted earlier and {{undated}} postings show no date. Older weeks are undercounted: roles that closed before the board began never made it in. The current week is still filling up.

The history lives in data/history.csv, rebuilt from the repository's history by scripts/stats.py. Job data is CC0; code is MIT.
